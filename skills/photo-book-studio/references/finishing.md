# Finishing modes

Read only the relevant section. Local corrections do not trigger full-book review or print export.

## Whole-book review

Render every current spread for an explicitly requested full review or finalization. Inspect consecutive groups of four to six; open flagged spreads at normal preview size. Check story rhythm, important portraits, repeated silhouettes, photo-linked copy, quote punctuation across pages and opening/ending coherence. Do not add transformations, stickers or chapter pages simply because a route has been used less often.

Inspect computed positions and actual background colors. Background layers must not change the positioning context of dates, captions, stickers or letters. Check supporting text separately: inherited muted colors and reduced sizes can make it unreadable even when the main caption passes. Renderer-wide changes require review of every affected spread.

Save current page count, inspected spreads, fixes, completeness and unresolved limits in the project audit. Replace stale findings after revisions; a former blank writing page may now contain a letter. Cache evidence, then rerender changed pages only for later local corrections.

## Opening and closing letters

Use paragraph-aware letter layout, not caption markup that shrinks each line after the first. Preserve approved wording and signature alignment. Give decorations their own reading-free region. Measure text and signature after fonts load at normal preview size and intended print size. Fixed screen-pixel offsets do not prove print placement. Longer copy may need another page rather than smaller type.

## Print planning and delivery

Calculate generated-art needs from physical size: pixels = millimetres / 25.4 × target DPI. A 210mm square needs approximately 2480px at 300 DPI before bleed. Request suitable resolution when the generator supports it; otherwise record the limit early. Cover lettering baked into raster art needs particular attention. Enlarging pixels alone does not recover detail; compare any higher-resolution version against the approved design.

Review effective DPI at fitted/cropped size for photos, derivatives, covers and major backgrounds. Distinguish browser estimates from final PDF measurements. Keep weak-resolution photos intentional, report their limits, and consider smaller supporting placement only when the memory still receives appropriate space.

Use printer specifications for final cover/spine dimensions and binding-dependent safety. Count individual interior faces separately from covers; confirm the printer's page increment. Export the requested single pages or spreads. Browser default print CSS is not automatically a production PDF.

Before delivery, verify image loading, embedded fonts, physical type size, bleed, trim/gutter safety, cover/spine template and page count. An attractive preview, successful HTML build or complete photo placement does not mean print preflight passed. Preserve the approved design and report remaining limits.

Default final-cover generation occurs after the interior and whole-book review. Samples use a placeholder. Use actual story, palette and user-approved reference assets; make clear whether image generation is available. Do not inherit the original author's permissions or cover art.

For long text, propose opening/ending writing pages before reducing type. Offer story-specific image-generated writing paper, such as a departure route connecting meaningful places, only with a defined calm reading field. Measure paragraphs against the actual generated background and preserve the user's words. Event emphasis can also use a photo-derived torn background behind regular photos/text, instead of a standalone framed poster.
