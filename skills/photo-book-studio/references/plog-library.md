### Acquired material families

- [Maki by Mapbox](https://github.com/mapbox/maki): CC0 travel/activity pictograms. Eighteen were acquired in the private study, covering transport, meals, parks, cinema, music, museums and other outings. These are graphic map/stamp motifs, not hand-painted stickers.
- [Kenney Board Game Icons](https://kenney.nl/assets/board-game-icons): CC0 gaming symbols. Sixteen selected SVGs were acquired, with bounds and charcoal-fill derivatives for practical use; original files and license were retained.
- Original vector utilities in the private study: twelve paper/tape/label/pocket/frame/arrow/moon/heart assets. They can provide structural material instead of another photo card.
- [FreeSVG](https://freesvg.org/): selected movie/travel pages declared CC0/public domain. Attempted download endpoints returned no usable SVG, and normal page fetching encountered a restriction. Keep these as researched candidates, not acquired files.
- [Transparent Textures](https://www.transparenttextures.com/): discovery only; a satisfactory current license was not established, so no textures were acquired.

The private starter collection has 46 acquired/created SVG assets, all checked in a browser gallery. No private workspace paths, third-party screenshots or raw asset pack are bundled into the skill. A fresh user can fetch suitable items from the credited sources or use original vector utilities; the skill must not assume this author's local collection exists.

The material gap remains: these graphic symbols and vector paper tools do not constitute a rich library of high-quality hand-painted illustration kits. Add theme-specific illustration sources and test their actual fit as needed; do not inflate the collection count with near-duplicate icons or count a source visit as a ready asset.

### Generated paper families

A private study generated two structural-material families with the built-in image tool: creamy travel-paper textures and bright outing-paper textures. Each has eight blank objects: writing paper, ticket, assembled pocket, envelope, paired washi strips, graph strip, label and arrow/star cluster. Sixteen alpha PNGs were extracted and checked on light/dark backgrounds; one neighboring-piece contamination was corrected. These are reusable material samples, not proof that a finished Plog is better.

When expanding such a set, specify the function, blank writing region, material behavior, palette and isolation gutters. Avoid invented ticket numbers/dates, readable faux brands, heavy outer shadows and an entire scene behind the assets. Record tool/prompt and sheet-to-piece provenance. Preserve suitable existing material; generating more variants is optional and does not create a requirement to use a new set in every page. Choose source assets for repeated ordinary needs and generation for a useful missing style or relationship.
