# Seven optional creative treatments

Assess these seven routes after reading the photographs and brief, before locking composition in a new album batch or substantial redesign. Story groups and page estimates are provisional at this point. Initial browser review later tests the chosen candidates; it is not the first suitability assessment. For standalone Plog, use the short workflow: the seven routes are a quick suitability menu, and only selected candidates/important constraints need written decisions. Ordinary album batch review still assesses all seven. They are also available for an explicitly requested Plog/手账. In Plog, use them as an insert, decorative source-derived asset, or an explicitly requested alternative composition; the ordinary photo/handwriting/material workflow remains primary. Local wording/crop corrections do not trigger a new full review.

## Intake and content rules

The first visible production step for a new ordinary album is the fixed questionnaire in `intake-questionnaire.md`. Complete it and persist the answers before ingesting or composing. Only a standalone Plog uses its smaller intake. Respect saved permissions for art derivatives, torn edges, simplification and generated decoration. A seven-route review does not grant new permissions.

Every selected photo remains intentional. Do not inherit another skill's duplicate omission or automatic destination-object rules. Extra artwork is decorative by default, with source relationships recorded. A replacement requires explicit approval and retains its original source file ID. A generated collage containing three source photos is not automatically three auditable photo placements: retain the originals in the normal layout or build the collage with truthful individual photo layers. Never hide missing photos behind a generation result.

## The seven routes

| Route | Useful situation | Required visual relationship / rejection check |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Minimal Zine Poster | Cover, chapter pause, a source-linked phrase or single motif | A quiet field and one structural accent; no unrelated invented story. Its minimalism is specific to this route, not the whole book. |
| 2. Photo Abstract Editorial | A single strong photo with geometry, distance or repetition | Keep a faithful photo beside a separate source-derived memory panel. Reject a repainted source or an unrelated abstract panel. |
| 3. Gathered Scenes | People and environment jointly carry a memory | Integrate a truthful photo anchor with source-derived illustration through an irregular fibrous tear. Reject two complete pictures merely stacked. |
| 4. Photo to Zine Postcard | A day, meal, place, object or chapter insert | Preserve the photo; add a source-specific small motif and useful card/back structure. Its native postcard ratio does not dictate album dimensions. |
| 5. Travel Envelope | Travel, birthdays, meals, dates, gatherings, exhibitions or everyday fragments benefit from a pocket/correspondence cluster; travel facts are not required for a Plog pocket adaptation | Recognizable source scenery around a thin paper envelope; asymmetrical overlapping photo/cutout contents disappear behind the front pocket. Avoid an opaque oversized box, invented souvenirs, repeated sources or an envelope physically standing on photographed pavement. |
| 6. Subject Breaks the Frame | A recognizable contour can cross a photographic window | Clean warm-white canvas, straight rectangular photo window containing real source context, and a truthful source subject on a higher layer crossing the edge. Reject a mere border, invented anatomy/branches, duplicate subjects, repainted illustration or floating-card shadow. |
| 7. Three-photo Paper Collage | Three related photos benefit from a quiet layered keepsake cluster | Portrait 3:4, one central main photograph, one landscape and one portrait support, about 45% collage occupancy, warm-white breathing room; 10–15 thin paper/vellum/notebook/envelope/color layers. Derive colors from photos, avoid automatic black tape, and add no text/logos/watermarks. One small plant or flower may accent the adapted component without competing with photographs. Reject dominant filler flowers/objects, three similar portrait cards or materials that consume all whitespace. |

Routes 5–7 have self-contained guidance and the two user-supplied prompts in `creative-prompts.md`. Route 5 is an independently written adaptation informed by the credited travel-envelope repository; it does not require installing that skill. The other specialized skills remain optional asset tools. Follow the active image tool's schema and inspect local input images before generation. Attach the actual selected original photos to the imagegen call with the complete filled prompt. Generate photo/paper relationships as one artwork by default, including isolated local modules when suitable. Do not silently substitute blank-material generation and manual photo placement; read the input/fallback guidance in `creative-prompts.md`.

## Match scenarios before committing to a layout

Read these as opportunities, not an automatic mapping from activity to style. For delegated handmade Plog, ordinary relationship-led composition plus final imagegen implementation is the normal path. Consider routes 5–7 early when they add a specific grouping/focal benefit, but do not require an effect trial for every matching source set; consult recent treatments in the current project to avoid repeated envelope/stack templates; they are structural composition options, not last-minute embellishments. All seven remain available, and zero uses remains valid.

- **Envelope / pocket:** useful when several moments belong together but their aspect ratios or backgrounds differ, when a small sequence should feel collected, or when a main photo and smaller details need one shared anchor. Birthdays, meals, pets, family gatherings, entertainment and everyday outings can all fit. Let the front pocket hide nonessential lower edges, with prints emerging at uneven heights. A scenic photograph is needed for the full scenic travel-envelope composition, but not for a pocket layered on a paper Plog background. Do not invent travel, tickets, souvenirs or correspondence facts. Evaluate silhouette and scale: a large flat box or aggressive occlusion can weaken the page.
- **Subject breaks the frame:** useful when a visible branch, building, flower bouquet, pet contour, person or gesture can bridge the photo and surrounding paper. Choose an edge whose crossing adds direction or emphasis, with source context still legible. Trees and architecture are proven promising examples, not restrictions. Preserve interaction and anatomy; skip an unsafe cutout rather than inventing hidden structure. A partial crossing can suffice; extracting the whole person is not required.
- **Three-photo paper cluster:** useful for person + environment + detail, three related actions, or three visually compatible fragments that can share a clear main/support hierarchy. Compare grouping before declaring the route unsuitable; an existing four-photo page is not a reason by itself to reject a three-photo cluster. Keep the remaining photograph truthfully placed elsewhere on that page or another justified page. Two source photos do not authorize duplicating one or inventing a third. The exact complete route retains its 3:4, occupancy and no-text prompt constraints; borrowing its overlap hierarchy for a larger Plog is a local adaptation, not a claim to have executed the complete route.

For a promising match, compare the ordinary concept with a concrete creative alternative: identify source IDs, focal role, overlap/occlusion order, intended scale and the benefit to the story. A small layout sketch or faithful layer draft is enough to choose initially; do not generate seven trial artworks. If a source-specific raster treatment is necessary and permitted, use imagegen to test the strongest candidate. SVG, masks and approved existing materials remain valid. Record the selected approach or a specific rejection in the existing creative review. When recording a local adaptation, name which relationship was borrowed and which full-route constraints were not applied.

Rejecting a candidate needs a source, permission, tool-fidelity or composition reason. “The layout is already finished”, “the group currently has four photos”, “it occupies space” or “this is not a trip” alone do not establish unsuitability. Compare regrouping or scale first. Do not force a treatment when it harms legibility, authenticity or an explicit user preference, and do not treat this comparison as permission for photo replacement.

## Actual image inputs — albums and Plog

The actual-source-input rule applies to every photo-derived treatment in ordinary albums as well as Plog, regardless of which optional creative skill is invoked. Supply original image references and the actual filled prompt. For a later finishing edit, supply the intermediate artwork as the target with needed original fidelity references; do not let repeated generations turn previous generated faces into the only source. Pure symbolic decorations/material utilities may be reference-free. Preserve permissions, original IDs, fidelity review and print requirements; this does not automatically add a Plog finishing pass to ordinary albums.

## Source suitability before generation

Compare source candidates for the intended relationship: light, calm/busy regions, palette compatibility, contour quality, spatial layers and the prospective photographic hierarchy. Being technically extractable is not enough to make a strong showcase. Trees, branches, landscapes and buildings often make a subtle frame breakout; an animal or face is not prohibited, but do not choose one merely because it is an obvious cutout. An unsuitable treatment candidate remains in the normal album; never discard a selected memory for aesthetic weakness.

For the reference-led three-photo paper route, compose one compact overlapped unit: a clear main photo in front, a landscape and a portrait support partly visible behind, and paper layers binding the same group. Do not treat all three photos as equal independent cards or put them in separate columns. The main photo can remain rectangular; subject extraction is an optional reference-led variation, not a requirement for all collages. Calmer paper, subdued support photos and fewer competing edges may better match the user's reference. Assess the whole silhouette and breathing room before accepting.

## Choosing, generating and reviewing

Record a compact `creativeReview`: each route's use/skip/candidate decision and the reason tied to this event, permission and available assets/tools. Ordinary album review assesses all seven; standalone Plog records its shortlist and significant constraints. Using zero is valid. For an ordinary batch, zero to two strong treatments is a useful starting point, not a quota. Explicitly requested studies or a whole-page style may use more. Do not mechanically impose a content percentage on a requested Plog composition, nor use all seven for variety.

Preserve an approved composition and its useful accents during revisions. A correction can rebuild composition when that solves the actual problem; it is not a mandatory fresh-start step every time. Evaluate the result against its source, layout intent and normal viewing size. More pages, more materials, more tool calls or greater sparsity do not prove better design.

Generated derivatives are not pixel-exact. For ordinary Plog, use one final normal-size content/expression review, not per-generation enlarged face/texture comparisons or pixel-difference analysis. Consult a source only to diagnose an obvious final mistake. Ordinary albums retain their applicable source/print checks. If unchanged original pixels are required, use faithful photo layers and masks; do not promise imagegen is pixel-exact. Keep the source files read-only and do not invent missing anatomy or scenery. If a specific cutout cannot be extracted faithfully with available tools, retain a framed photo or skip that route.

For imagegen, save the actual tool, prompt, selected source map and output; inspect before accepting. One focused correction is generally enough to test a candidate. Disclose remaining deviations instead of retrying indefinitely or certifying every numeric ratio by eye. External references are inspiration, not an asset reuse license.

Finish by rendering every changed page, checking identity/occurrence counts and reviewing actual pixels. A generated material may be reused only after alpha, crop and source/provenance checks. PDF output remains opt-in.
