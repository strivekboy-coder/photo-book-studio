# Photographic narrative and spatial relationships

Read for a calm photographic book, a photo-led magazine, or a reference built around panoramic anchors, floating insets, support columns and quiet text fields. These relationships also work in travel, couple and family books; this is not an exclusive theme or required zine direction.

## Read the reference

Gray image placeholders, upload arrows, dotted gutter guides and example captions are tutorial instructions, not decorations to copy into the finished book. Extract visual relationships and recompose with the user's source geometry and story. Do not inherit weddings, places or emotional facts from example copy.

## Useful relationships

- **Panoramic scene with a floating inset:** a scene anchors both pages; a bordered image bridges a quiet region and the scene. The inset adds action, time or a detail rather than merely repeating the background. Keep it clear of faces, landmarks and the physical gutter. A thin border separates tones; a stronger border can imply a collected print.
- **Environmental hero opposite a compact detail group:** give the place or moment enough scale, then connect two or three details with shared edges or an intentional rhythm. The support field needs whitespace. Important portraits must not shrink into miniature slots to imitate a reference.
- **Narrow photo column and a large photograph:** repeated small frames establish a reading path beside a hero and a separate text pocket. Adapt the number/proportions to content; rotate the relationship or add another spread when subjects cannot remain legible.
- **Photo opposite quiet text:** a complete intimate image may need no stickers or companion frames. Use a modest title and readable paragraphs; long copy can become a dedicated letter page. Empty space supplies pacing.
- **Quiet overlapping panels:** layering can feel natural in a clean book. Let a meaningful edge/overlap connect the panels while one remains dominant. Avoid unnecessary masks, rotation and heavy shadows.

## Geometry decides suitability

A cross-page slot is usually much wider than an ordinary landscape photograph; portraits rarely suit panoramas. Inspect the actual crop: environment, rooflines, gestures and people must remain meaningful. Adjust field height, crop position and inset position together. Filling the CSS frame proves nothing. If landmark and foreground cannot both survive, choose a taller field, another eligible source or a different composition. Never invent missing image area without permission.

Use the existing spread.backgroundPhoto contract with exactly one backgroundOnly source entry. Insets keep their own selected IDs; repeated rendering is not implied. Field heights/masking need explicit workspace CSS; unknown JSON fields do not automatically work. Sources remain read-only. Center seams/trim need a separate print review; a successful browser sample is not printer approval.

## Type and pacing

Give titles, supporting remarks and factual notes separate roles. Centering can create a pause; side text can preserve a tall portrait. Use separate notes for long or equally ranked sentences where caption newlines would shrink later lines. Keep readable size and avoid lone final characters. Serif or sans systems are both valid; decorative English metadata is optional.

An open portrait, scene with inset, detail cluster and quiet pause are available operations, not a compulsory sequence. Choose from actual aspect ratio, visual mass, source resolution and importance.

## Worked study and review

The independently recomposed [有风的日子 study](https://strivekboy-coder.github.io/photo-book-studio/showcase/photographic-story/) uses 12 previously authorized stock photographs, each once, across four body spreads. The initial panorama cut away too much river; increasing field height and adjusting crop retained church rooflines and river context. Its inset avoids the skyline. Figures use contain rather than destructive crops; supporting photographs leave an independent text field.

Review scene completeness, inset obstruction, primary/secondary scale, reading path, empty-space purpose, body text and gutter safety. Judge source-fit before approving a reference effect. This is a same-agent worked example, not proof of independent-model performance.
