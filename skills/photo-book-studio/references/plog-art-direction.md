# Plog visual direction before coordinates

Optional visual guidance for a new standalone Plog or substantial redesign when its direction needs clarification. Start with `plog-workflow.md`; this is not another mandatory stage. This is a short design pass, not another questionnaire, asset quota or seven-image generation task.

## Look at pixels, not summaries alone

When relying on a local visual example, open it with the available image viewer; links/text alone are not viewing. Original photos or user references may provide a sufficient direction without consulting every example. Pair a relevant example with the user's actual photographs. Study relationships, not copied content. The reference people, wording, scenery and coordinates never become the new user's memories. These examples illustrate distinct possibilities, not one mandatory style or proof of reproducibility.

- `../assets/plog-visual-references/approved-night.jpg`: the approved original night direction, with masked faces. Study a bright emotional accent, handwritten remarks close to specific snapshots, varied border treatments and stars/lines on a scene-colored field. The large repeated portraits are intentional here; not every page needs a tiny subordinate photograph.
- `../assets/plog-visual-references/paper-cluster.jpg`: a complete restrained three-photo treatment. Study partial occlusion and the shared cluster silhouette. Its large quiet surround and no-text prompt are route-specific, not ordinary Plog defaults.
- `../assets/plog-visual-references/frame-breakout.jpg`: a nature-based contour crossing. Study the truthful context inside the window and source contour outside; do not select any easy cutout merely to demonstrate the effect.
- `../assets/plog-visual-references/plain-ruled-paper.svg`: original reusable plain stationery. This is a material illustration, not a finished-photo sample. Everyday exercise-book paper is a valid canvas or strip: faint blue rulings, red margin, grid or dots can support lively photographs and writing without vintage texture.

For a direction absent from these examples, actually inspect a relevant user/creator work when accessible. An honest offline fallback is allowed, but do not present cached prose as a viewed visual reference. Further research should answer a design question, not collect more homepage URLs.

## Make one coherent visual idea

Before writing fixed x/y coordinates, briefly identify:

1. **Personal observation:** a visible gesture, expression, object, contrast, repeated action or user-supplied joke worth marking. Distinguish factual notes from clearly symbolic mood. Do not invent relationships, dates, events, possessions or quotations.
2. **Page relationship:** what organizes this memory? Possibilities include an ordinary ruled sheet interrupted by snapshots and pen notes, a grid-paper spine joining meal details, a scene-backed night cluster, a pocket that gathers fragments, or a source contour crossing a frame. Pick for these photographs; none is a default.
3. **Joint construction:** decide photo proportions, lettering placement, material roles and expressive accents together. A sticker may set a focal point; a paper strip may determine alignment; a caption may determine the eye path. Do not finish photo slots and then decorate leftover corners.

When a substantial first design would otherwise default to upright/tilted white-bordered cards and header/footer captions, compare a materially different compact concept before committing. A short sketch or layer draft suffices. Produce the stronger candidate, not several finished variants unless requested. Seven-route matching belongs inside this design pass; it does not replace ordinary expressive composition.

## Facts are protected; symbolic decoration is free

Keep source photographs and user facts truthful. Decorations may respond to mood, shape, color, play, affection or atmosphere even if their subject is absent from the photograph. Hearts, stars, moon marks, flowers, colorful squiggles and expressive stamps can belong without claiming they were present. A clearly decorative icon is different from a realistic invented ticket containing a supposed visit, price or date.

Source-linked means responsive, not literal duplication. A hand gesture can become an expressive line; a color can become a tab; an affectionate moment can receive a bright heart. Do not draw only a small copy of the photographed toy, cake or animal beside each frame. Free association must help the composition rather than become arbitrary filler. Neither every ornament touching a photo nor one sticker per photograph is required.

## Ordinary paper can carry an expressive page

Handmade does not automatically mean yellowed parchment, cotton-rag pockets, stains, thick torn edges or three-dimensional props. Plain white/off-white ruled, grid or dotted homework/notebook paper can be the entire background or one narrow writing strip. Let photographic shapes, nearby notes, varied scale and a small color accent create life. Use subtle grain only if useful. Avoid turning clean paper into another yellow-beige vintage pack by habit.

A pocket is appropriate only when its opening, inserted prints and front lip create a convincing collection relationship. A broad rectangle with a headline is a title label, not automatically a successful envelope composition. Preserve useful materials, but do not repeat one generated front pocket as every page's label, foundation and structural idea. Cohesion can come from shared ink or lettering with different paper forms. If the user requests plain school paper, honor it directly without forcing pocket or torn-paper effects.

## Connect gestures and words naturally

Read posture, gaze, hand direction, held objects and repeated actions before placing notes. Carry motion through height changes, staggered edges, size, repeated color, or a line that meaningfully points to the source detail. An arrow joining two generic empty corners is not automatically action continuity. Do not add arrows everywhere or draw lines across faces/hands. A playful line, underline, circle or short remark may be enough; preserve key subjects.

Use actual-copy natural-handwriting specimens and nearby, specific draft notes instead of default event heading + explanatory subheading + folio. Typography may remain tidy if the reference calls for it. Do not fabricate dialogue or mimic a personal memory the user never supplied. Intentional asymmetry and uneven note rhythm are welcome; arbitrary per-character jitter is not a handwriting substitute.

## Choose implementation after the concept

For photo-derived imagegen methods, pass the actual original images with the complete filled prompt and generate the photo/material relationship together; read `creative-prompts.md#pass-source-images-with-photo-derived-prompts`. Do not default to generating blank paper and placing photos yourself. Pure materials and symbolic/native SVG remain valid separate assets. Use the standard layer editor when editable HTML is requested; image-only integrated artworks do not need an editable-layer reconstruction. Use faithful photo crops/masks, original SVG, acquired materials and imagegen as needed. Do not let the easiest rectangle in the renderer decide the concept. A textured motif may benefit from generation; a plain ruled page and expressive arrows often do not. Build lower materials, original photographic layers and foreground attachments as a coordinated assembly. Avoid a detached generated backdrop with complete photo cards simply pasted on top.

Let final imagegen implement suitable cutouts, crossings and markings from the planned relationship rather than defaulting to custom native mask code. If an explicit exact-pixel constraint makes a desired cutout unsupported, explain that limitation and use a faithful simpler native relationship. Never repaint a person to satisfy the concept, silently replace originals, or claim a circle crop is subject extraction. Preserve editor functionality. Full-route derivative permissions still apply.

## Judge expressiveness separately from integrity

After rendering, first check sources, crops and readability. Then judge: does it feel like someone reacting to these particular moments, or a generic album template? Do notes, paper, photographs and ornaments form a relationship? Is there a confident scale/color/material choice suited to the brief? Does the paper actually bind or frame content rather than occupy spare space? Are adjacent pages related without cloning the same construction?

Readable, intact photographs do not alone pass expressive review. Repeated cards and isolated icons can still be a base draft; the final image tool should implement the intended relationships. Judge the final result at normal full-page and phone sizes, not through per-generation pixel comparisons. Ordinary smoothing or an approved atmospheric foreground is not a reason to restore source pixels. Consult an original only to diagnose an obvious final content mistake or an explicit exact-pixel request. No style scores, decoration quotas or additional review forms are needed. The current project/authorized series should avoid repeatedly choosing the same large envelope or paper stack when a simpler relationship would work.
